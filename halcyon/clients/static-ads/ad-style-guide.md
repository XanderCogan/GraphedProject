# Halcyon Botanicals: ad style guide

Halcyon is fictional, so there is no site or Ad Library to pull from. This guide is written from the customer brief and the ad names in the account, and it is the single reference every render uses.

## Colors

- Fern `#2F4A3A`: primary, backgrounds and wordmark
- Cream `#F4EBDD`: paper, light backgrounds, body text on fern
- Fig `#8C3B4A`: one accent per ad, never more
- Gold `#C9A24E`: thin rules and small details only
- No pure black, no pure white, no gradients

## Type

- Headline: a high-contrast editorial serif (Canela or Freight Display feel), title case, tight leading, 3 to 8 words
- Sub-line and CTA: a plain geometric sans (Inter or Söhne feel), sentence case
- Wordmark: HALCYON in the serif, letterspaced, small, top center. One line, no icon.

## Layout

- 4:5 portrait. One product, one message, one CTA.
- Bottle front-lit in late-afternoon light; a single garnish (fig half, rosemary sprig, orange twist). No tablescapes, no crowds.
- Headline in the top third or bottom third, never over the bottle label.
- CTA is a cream pill with fern text, or fern pill with cream text, bottom center.
- Generous margins. If it feels sparse, it is right.

## Copy rules

- Say what it is: non-alcoholic aperitif, adaptogens, no sugar. Never "mocktail".
- One claim per ad. Numbers beat adjectives ("0g sugar", "15% off every month").
- No medical claims, no "cures", no "detox". Adaptogens "help you unwind", nothing more.
- Price points that exist: $38 to $42 a bottle, 3-pack $99, subscription 15% off.
- CTA verbs: Shop, Subscribe, Send one. Never "Learn more".

## Imagery

- Warm, editorial, slightly underexposed. Think a Texas porch at 6 pm, not a studio.
- Real glass, real condensation, real shadows. No people's faces (the UGC ads already own that).
- Never: robots, glowing effects, drop shadows, stock-photo smiles, cocktail umbrellas.

## What the current ads already cover

Prospecting, from the account's 17 ads: occasion (Golden Hour UGC, Zero Proof Dinner Party, Dinner Party Lifestyle), identity (Sober Curious Testimonial), pain (Hangover-Free Hook), proof (Founder Story, v2), how-to (Mocktail Recipe Reel, Pour Shot), season (Fall Fig, Summer Spritz), ingredients (Ingredient Flatlay, Adaptogen Explainer, which ran four weeks at 0.85% CTR). Retargeting only: reviews (Review Carousel 4.8 stars), subscription (Subscribe & Save 15%), bundle (Trio Bundle Offer). Nothing cold ever sees a price, a sugar number, the subscription, a gift, or a comparison to wine. New ads go there.
